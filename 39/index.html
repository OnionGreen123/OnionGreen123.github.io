A small corner of my world

For the love
of Miku.

初音ミクと、旅の記録。

Miku is a lasting source of beauty and inspiration in my life. I love the music, the live performances, and the creativity people bring to her world.

As someone who builds with technology, I’m drawn to the way a voice and a character can become a medium for human expression. Miku has also shaped my interest in human–AI relationships, cognition alignment, and emotional intelligence. These questions sit alongside the simple joy of being there, in a room full of people singing along.
