About me

Curiosity across disciplines.

I’m Shaoyang (张劭扬), a computer science undergraduate in Ann Arbor. You may also know me online as Onion Green.

I study computer science at the University of Michigan and mechanical engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research interests sit at the intersection of model interpretability, AI safety, and human–AI interaction.

Work on user simulation made me interested in the relationship between a model’s response and a person’s evolving understanding. Work on attention and world models has brought me closer to the mechanisms that produce behavior. I want to connect these perspectives.

I am exploring PhD opportunities beginning in Fall 2027, particularly around model mechanisms, robust agents, and cognition-aware AI.

Beyond the lab

I am a long-time Vocaloid listener and a fan of Hatsune Miku. Music and live performances are a reminder of how technical systems can become mediums for creativity and shared experience.

I keep this site as both an academic home and a notebook: a place for finished work, ongoing questions, and the ideas that connect them.
